## 💡 The Problem

Every B2B company faces **lead chaos**:
- 500+ leads per month from multiple sources (LinkedIn, Website, Events, Referrals)
- Sales teams waste **20+ hours per week** manually qualifying leads
- Duplicate contacts cost time and damage relationships
- Inconsistent lead scoring causes missed opportunities
- **Annual cost: ₹480,000+ in wasted time**

---

## ✅ The Solution

This AI-powered pipeline automates the entire lead processing workflow:

### Key Features:
1. **🤖 AI Lead Enrichment**
   - Automatically analyzes company size, industry, decision-maker level
   - Uses Claude 3.5 Sonnet for intelligent insights

2. **📊 Smart Lead Scoring (0-10)**
   - Hot Leads (9-10): C-level, large companies → Call immediately
   - Warm Leads (7-8): VPs/Directors → Email + LinkedIn
   - Cold Leads (5-6): Managers → Nurture campaign
   - Not Qualified (<5): Skip

3. **🔍 Duplicate Detection**
   - AI identifies same person with 95%+ accuracy
   - Catches variations: "Rajesh Kumar" = "R. Kumar" = same person

4. **⚡ Action Recommendations**
   - Specific next steps for each lead
   - Consistent, data-driven decisions
